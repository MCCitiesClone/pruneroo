Inspector
Department Guide​


Role
As an Inspector, you’re a member of the Department of Construction and Transportation. Your primary role is to inspect privately owned plots and make reports against properties that violate property regulations and laws.

Making Reports
Reports are made on the Eviction Forum.

(1) Click the report button in the top right hand corner

(2) Enter the plot number into the ‘Plot Number’ box in which you are wanting to report.

(3) Enter the eviction date - the current date + the amount of days the eviction requires.

(4) Enter the plot owner - found using /as info region (the second line of the message, NOT the landlord)

(5) Select a report reason from the dropdown menu from the eviction policy.

(6) Write how to solve their report. There are some pre-generated options below:

Report TypeHow to Resolve
EyesoreReview your build and address any points listed in the report. For tailored guidance, you may open a DCT ticket on Discord. Once changes are made, post screenshots in your eviction report to have it marked as "solved by owner" and avoid eviction.
Lack of ProgressContinue working on your build and ensure meaningful progress is made. Share screenshots in the report thread. Once progress is deemed significant (e.g., completed floor or shell), the report will be marked as "solved by owner." Keep progressing to prevent future reports.
Non-ComplianceUpdate your build to meet zoning and thematic requirements. If you are unsure what needs to be corrected, open a DCT ticket for assistance. Once revised, post screenshots in the report thread to resolve the report.
Rental LimitationsImmediate eviction applies if rental rules were broken. For further clarification or dispute, open a DCT ticket.
InactivityYou have seven days to meet the minimum 6-hour playtime requirement. If you're unable to meet this due to valid reasons, you may request a deferral via the deferral request.
Plot FairnessReduce your plot and rental holdings to comply with legal limits. Post a screenshot of your /as me menu in the report thread to resolve the report as "solved by owner."
Skybridges / GroundbridgesCheck that your skybridge/groundbridge meets permit and size requirements. If not, open a DCT ticket to request the necessary changes or a new permit. Once fixed, your report can be resolved.
Food TruckEnsure your food truck has a valid permit and complies with any design or location requirements. Use a DCT ticket to request help or a new permit if needed.

(7) Attach the corresponding evidence to your report. This will vary depending on the report but all reports must include:

(a) Evidence that the player owns the plot (screenshot of the /as info region or the players /as me)

(b) Remaining evidence from the Evidence Required table below

Report TypeEvidence Required

Lack of ProgressIf Not Started: Logs (request via staff /ticket) or timestamped photo from 14 days prior.
If Started: Screenshots 14+ days apart showing no significant progress

All ReportsMust include proof of ownership and reply with proof of notice

(8) Check the Player Rights box

(9) Hit the Submit button

(10) Send an eviction notice to the owner of the plot:

(a) /dct-eviction-notice add (username) (region) (link to report)

(b) Don't include the brackets when you run the command. Every part of the command is case sensitive so be careful.

(c) Attach (by editing or replying to your report) with a screenshot confirming the eviction notice has been sent.

Solving a Report
Upon the date of the eviction, an Inspection Officer+ will continue processing the report through the eviction tree. Once the report has been either staff actioned, auctioned, or resolved, the 'Solved' tag will be applied by an Inspection Officer+. You will be paid when the tag changes from pending.

Resetting Notices
Once an eviction report you filed is solved, it is your responsibility to run /dct-reset-plot-status <region> to reset all notices!

Commands
/dct-tp <region> <reason> - used to teleport to plots for BI purposes only, this is heavily monitored for abuse
/about <user> - gives you information about specified user (playtime, jobs, etc.)
/as me <user> - lists every regioned the specified user owns
/as info region <plot name> - gives information about a plot
/kit regionbook - gives you a regionbook, clicking this book anywhere will autorun /as info region
/as setowner <user> <plot name> - sets the owner of a plot (mainly for leadership use)

Pay
You'll receive pay in accordance with the Department Payment Policy.

Channels
#relations - you can let us know that you will be away, if not you can be fired (see below for activity requirements)
#town-relations - any questions you have for Mayors about town plots
#commands - where TP and other commands are logged to track abuse
#plots - where concerns and or evidence are stored.

Activity Requirements
The Inspection Team must meet the playtime requirement of 6 hours a month. Aim to complete six reports/month, however this is not a quota.

If you are going away for an extended period, you can advice the department in your relations channel.

Trainee Inspector -> Inspector
You must complete 10 correct reports and ping the Inspection Manager in your channel and they will double check your reports and then update your roles if you have completed this.

Correct as at 28 Jun 25
